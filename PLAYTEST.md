# 实玩与验证 / Playtest and validation

## 中文

五位全新AI评审通过实际浏览器完成11局、244次玩家决定，原版本加权均分80.8/100，单人77.6–85。七维分别为趣味、人物代入、选择公平、惊喜回响、节奏可读性、音画交互、重玩差异。原分未达到当时85分的内部目标，保留原分，不把后续修改直接换算成涨分。它不是人类用户抽样研究或外部奖项认定。

最终版本根据反馈补了失权后的家庭/公共责任抉择、准确的战术准备与本人致命风险、两帝合守的可见结果。完整剧情包含118节点、214条连接、26终局；已提供可重放的逐边和终局见证。最终工程与定向浏览器验证记录和原始评分分开，不宣称全部路径均由真人或浏览器逐一游玩。

已知边界：第二遍前段仍有共同骨架；窄屏的军情说明较密；正文尚无英文翻译；未做微信真机验收。真实运行配乐录音已确认出声、无满幅削波，但本次AI评审未实际试听音乐，听感质量不在评分证据范围。版本按完整交付收尾，不再为了内部评分反复扩写。

## English

Five fresh AI playtesters used the actual browser game for 11 runs and 244 player decisions. The original weighted mean was 80.8/100, with individual scores from 77.6 to 85. The seven dimensions were enjoyment, characters and immersion, choice fairness, surprises and callbacks, pacing and readability, audiovisual interaction, and replay variety. The original score was below the then-target of 85. It is preserved rather than increased on the assumption that revisions improved it. This is neither a human-user study nor an external award assessment.

The final revision adds a family-versus-public-duty decision after losing political power, precise preparation and mortal-risk warnings, and visible consequences for the joint border-defense order. The complete story has 118 nodes, 214 connections, and 26 endings, with reproducible edge and ending witnesses. Engineering checks and focused browser checks are separate from the original subjective scores; not every route was manually played through in a browser.

Known limitations: early events share a common structure across runs; briefing text can be dense on short screens; in-game text is not yet translated into English; no physical-device WeChat testing was performed. A real runtime audio recording confirmed nonzero output without full-scale clipping, but the AI reviewers did not listen to the music, so musical quality is outside their evidence. The release closes the complete delivery rather than repeatedly expanding it to chase an internal score.
